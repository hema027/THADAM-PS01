Classic Slow-Simmered Tomato Basil Soup

Nothing compares to a steaming bowl of homemade soup on a quiet rainy afternoon. Today we are exploring the subtleties of fresh vine-ripened tomatoes, sweet aromatic basil, and a touch of roasted garlic butter.

Ingredients List

  • 2 lbs ripe plum tomatoes, quartered
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup fresh sweet basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin cold-pressed olive oil
  • 1 cup vegetable broth & sea salt to taste

Gently roast the tomatoes with olive oil and garlic at 375°F until slightly caramelized. Puree until silky smooth and finish with freshly torn basil. Serve with warm artisan sourdough bread.
